Scale and lore assumptions:
1 scene unit = 1 kilometre. Physical mesh dimensions and distances are linear.
Screen-space dots and labels are navigation markers, not physical body sizes.
Planets use Belter Charts ephemerides at 2356-01-01, an illustrative story epoch.
Tycho is mobile in the Belt. Ceres +60 degrees is a fan placement convention.
Its 750 m overall span is approximate. Anderson placement/size is illustrative.
The gate is fixed at Uranus' 19.19126393 AU semimajor axis +2 AU. Its azimuth
and inclination are illustrative, not known canonical XYZ coordinates.
Ring Space is a separate 1,000,000 km scene. The alien central station is 5 km;
Medina is the 2.46 km Nauvoo/Behemoth hull. Medina's local offset is illustrative.
The gate is 1,000 km across (TV convention); do not mix this with book dimensions.
Some upstream moon models retain approximate Kepler phases. Chart positions
remain fixed at the stated epoch; optional visual rotation does not advance it.
