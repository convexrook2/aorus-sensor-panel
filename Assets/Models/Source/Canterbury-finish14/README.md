# Accepted Canterbury finish14

Lennon accepted this reconstruction as sufficient for release on October 23,
2026. `owner-acceptance.json` retains his instruction and the exact Blender
and GLB identities. These are original authored models informed by Ryan Dening
concepts, Chris Danelon production views and the owner's television references;
no production mesh was acquired or television screenshots bundled here.

The GLB retains the accepted entrance, raised shroud, stepped housings, six aft
vessels and internal centerline reactor with radial attachments. Its twelve
materials use three embedded images. Nine physical outlets have separate
runtime plume registrations in `engines.json`; the Blender source also retains
its native volume effects. The six exported lights use COMPAT artistic units:
catalogue `lightUnitScale` is 1, followed by the normal model-fit conversion.

Hidden dimensions and small fittings remain estimates. Acceptance does not
claim exact production geometry. The ice cargo is deferred. Research and
earlier rejected revisions remain separate local material.

`python tools/Check-CanterburyAdoption.py` checks source, package and portrait
identity without rewriting the model. The Sensor's loading portrait is rendered
from this exact GLB and records its hash under `Assets/FleetViewer/portraits`.
