AORUS SENSOR PANEL - FAN MADE / ORIGINAL SOL NAVIGATION DIAGRAM

The native SOL / NAVIGATION schematic is restored from the panel's v1.7.1
implementation. Belter Charts supplies detailed asset pixels and geometry;
its web interface and Scene3D renderer are not loaded by the native panel.
The earlier web adaptation is preserved in NavigationWeb and its safety backup.

Asset source: Belter Charts by skylercole and contributors, Apache-2.0,
commit ba86075477fc529294a5e51cb4e1b0d183ff893a.
https://github.com/skylercole/belter-charts/tree/ba86075477fc529294a5e51cb4e1b0d183ff893a
See Belter-Charts-LICENSE.txt and Belter-Charts-Upstream-Credits.md.

Sun/planet maps, Earth cloud mask and Saturn ring strip: Solar System Scope,
CC BY 4.0. https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Galilean moon mosaics: NASA/JPL/USGS. Ceres: NASA/JPL-Caltech/UCLA/MPS/DLR/IDA.
The Ceres map uses the public-domain USGS Dawn global mosaic:
https://www.usgs.gov/media/images/global-mosaic-ceres-taken-during-dawn-mission
Eros shape: NEAR MSI team, NASA PDS Small Bodies Node, 89,398 source triangles.
Phoebe: Belter Charts' original procedural packed mesh and vertex colours.

Native adaptation: original image maps on UV-mapped spheres; the cloud mask
becomes alpha; the ring strip is mapped radially. Eros faces are retained;
source meshes are split into native render batches without simplification.
WPF approximates source materials. Packed vertex colours use face averages
in a native palette; the procedural web regolith shader is not reproduced.

Tycho, Medina and Sol gate: retained original panel detail geometry, exported
offline from NavigationWeb/src/models.ts. Three.js supplies the offline geometry
tools (MIT; three-LICENSE.txt). No paid, noncommercial-only or production-show
station mesh is bundled. The owner's Razorback v2 is a separate credited asset.

This is a SCHEMATIC, NOT TO SCALE. Models retain their shapes, while display
sizes and orbital spacing are enlarged/compressed for clarity. Positions and
motion are illustrative, not ephemerides or live spacecraft tracking.
Tycho is mobile in the Belt; its marker is an approximate placement.
The fixed Sol gate is shown outside Uranus and inside Neptune (~2 AU beyond
Uranus); its direction is illustrative. Medina, the former Nauvoo/Behemoth,
is in the separate labelled Ring Space inset, distinct from the alien central
Ring Station. The dashed portal link is a navigation relationship, not a distance.
