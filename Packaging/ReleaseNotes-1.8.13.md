# v1.8.13 - accepted Knight correction

The Knight showcase and holotable now load the accepted silver-hull review 04.
Version 1.8.12 mistakenly retained the earlier simplified Knight. This correction
adds the already accepted model without remodeling or changing its source files.

Knight retains its physical hull relief, silver finish, service wear, recreated
Pur'n'Kleen markings, seventeen work lights and three fitted canted drives with
compact white/violet plumes. Its loading and background portrait comes from the
same complete model used by the interactive renderer.

Canterbury, Arboghast and the other accepted ships retain their models, portraits
and drive registrations. Monitoring layout and preferences are unchanged.
This release makes no new hardware, game-FPS or GPU-budget claim. Fleet Studio
remains a separate application and is not installed by this Sensor update.

The reported AORUS Lite memory retention remains under investigation; this
Knight correction does not address it.
