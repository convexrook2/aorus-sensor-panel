# v1.8.10 — Anubis and fleet lighting

The sensor panel's fleet now includes the accepted Anubis assembly, with its
charcoal finish, recessed red clusters, blue light throughout the existing bow
interior and registered blue-white drive plume. The licensed multipart geometry,
normals, UVs and embedded surface map are retained without simplification.

Emitting ship lights now produce a shared selective glow in the native fleet
viewport. Ordinary paint and reflections retain their existing rendering.
The accepted Rocinante, other finished ships, monitoring layout and collection
behavior remain unchanged.

This release installs the sensor panel only. Fleet Studio remains a development
and reference-review tool. Editable Anubis source and CC BY 4.0 attribution are
retained in the repository; paid model assets and production reference images
are not distributed.

CI checks the exact pushed revision, model identities, existing-surface light
selection, shared glow controls, managed contracts and protected asset packages.
Published assets continue to use the authenticated RLAR v2 archive.
