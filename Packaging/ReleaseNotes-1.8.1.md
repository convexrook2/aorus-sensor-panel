# v1.8.1 — Correct ship and personnel associations

Corrects Epstein's yacht to Solomon Epstein, played by Sam Huntington, and
Donnager to Captain Theresa Yao, played by Jean Yoon. The Truman-class theme
identifies Admiral Michael Souther specifically with the Agatha King. Generic

updating the application's defaults alone does not replace local overrides.
No personal media pack is included in the public installer.
