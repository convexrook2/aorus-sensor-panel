# v1.8.9 — Finished Rocinante Season 6

The monitor and Fleet Studio include the owner-finished Rocinante: smooth
armour, paired relief markings, red existing lettering, consistent grey panel
finishes and a restrained bluish-grey sheen that responds to lighting.
The complete reviewed geometry and embedded maps are retained losslessly.

The single drive plume now starts at the physical bell outlet, with a bright
blue-white core and soft blue envelope. Existing service and navigation lights
remain intact. The new plume style is limited to this Rocinante revision;
other ships retain their current effects. The original Tachi remains a separate
Fleet Studio comparison.

Fleet Studio includes the on-demand eight-second light-pass recording and local
replay. Normal rotation, hidden/minimized lifecycle, monitoring layout and sensor
collection remain as before. Published creative assets continue to use the
authenticated RLAR v2 archive with readable notices; editable Blender source
is retained separately in the repository.

CI validates the exact pushed source revision, builds, contracts, full asset
hashes and protected packages. It does not install the update, launch live
sensors or publish a release.
