Adds **AORUS Dark** and **AORUS ICE**, with the original appearance retained as **AORUS Classic**. Theme changes update backgrounds, text, gauges, graphs and bars immediately and persist across restarts. Existing installations keep Classic until another theme is selected.

A small **settings gear** at the bottom-right opens a compact menu with a translucent, blurred background. Right-click opens the same menu. Display, theme, layout, scale, polling and FPS choices stay inside the menu rather than opening native popup submenus. Back returns to the previous page; Escape or clicking outside dismisses settings. The tray also offers **Open settings**.

The themes share the existing animated AORUS artwork and sensor collector. Selecting a theme does not reset readings, maxima, polling or display preferences. Fullscreen on a secondary monitor retains tray-only operation and the existing HDMI recovery behavior.

Visible saved-reading checks exercised the settings button handlers and all three themes at landscape, wide-strip and portrait preview sizes. Checks confirmed unchanged readings and an overlay bounded by the window. These checks exercise application navigation and rendering; they are not a claim of mouse-input validation on every physical display or DPI configuration.

Requires the shared Microsoft .NET 10 Desktop Runtime (Windows x64, stable 10.0.x), existing PawnIO 2.2+ for CPU/motherboard readings, and the installed NVIDIA driver for GPU readings. No Rainmeter or HWiNFO is required. Independent FAN MADE project; AORUS trademarks and supplied artwork belong to their owners.
