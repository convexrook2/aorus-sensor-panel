Adds eight themes: **Cyber Neon**, **Synthwave**, **Matrix Terminal**, **Carbon Gold**, **Crimson**, **Nord Aurora**, **Dracula** and **Mocha**. Classic, Dark and ICE remain available, bringing the collection to eleven themes.

Each new theme coordinates the background, text, graph grid, usage bars, metallic temperature rims and compact settings menu. Paired CPU/GPU accents and subtle grid, scanline, metal or colour-wash backgrounds give the themes distinct styles. The theme selector now shows palette swatches.

The original AORUS rim segments and curved lettering remain in every theme. Static vector backgrounds and recoloured rim images are cached and shared. No extra animation timer, downloaded theme extension or bitmap pack is added. Theme changes preserve readings and maxima. Upgrades preserve the selected theme, polling, startup, display, layout and scale settings; readings and session maxima start fresh when the app restarts.

Palette inspiration: [Nord](https://www.nordtheme.com/), [Dracula](https://draculatheme.com/contribute), [Catppuccin](https://catppuccin.com/palette/) and [SynthWave '84](https://github.com/robb0wen/synthwave-vscode). These are independent adaptations, not endorsed ports.

Validation covered unique theme IDs, persisted-ID lookup, frozen graphics resources and at least 4.5:1 foreground/muted-text contrast on each new panel/menu surface. Classic retains its original supplied colours. Saved-data walkthroughs covered landscape, wide-strip and portrait presentation, checking preserved sensor values and settings bounds. These checks do not establish native input or every physical display/DPI combination.

Requires the shared Microsoft .NET 10 Desktop Runtime (Windows x64, stable 10.0.x), existing PawnIO 2.2+ for CPU/motherboard readings, and the installed NVIDIA driver for GPU readings. Independent FAN MADE project; AORUS trademarks and supplied artwork belong to their owners.
