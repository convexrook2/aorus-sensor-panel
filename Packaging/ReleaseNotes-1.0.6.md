Fullscreen on a secondary sensor monitor now keeps the **FAN MADE tray icon** without a large taskbar button. The panel continues displaying live readings on its own screen, and the tray still provides Show panel, Fit to display, Hide to tray, README and Exit.

Restoring window size or moving the panel to the primary monitor restores its taskbar button. Taskbar visibility is applied during display placement, including startup, display switching and HDMI reconnect. Display diagnostics now report taskbar and tray visibility.

Validation covered build, collector and published panel checks, installed-file digests and fullscreen taskbar/tray state reported by the application. Diagnostic state is separate from visual taskbar verification and does not establish every display/DPI combination.

Requires **Microsoft .NET 10 Desktop Runtime, Windows x64, stable 10.0.x**. Setup offers the [Microsoft download](https://dotnet.microsoft.com/en-us/download/dotnet/10.0) if missing. CPU/motherboard readings require administrator access and the existing [PawnIO 2.2+ driver](https://pawnio.eu); NVIDIA GPU readings require the installed NVIDIA driver. Rainmeter and HWiNFO are not required.
